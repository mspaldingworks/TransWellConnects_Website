Welcome to Transpire

An interfaith network supporting our trans neighbors

Transpire is a free resource market for Louisville's trans community, hosted by a growing network of trans-affirming congregations and partners. Each market looks a little different, because what we offer transforms to meet what you actually need.

You are sacred. You are valued. You belong here.

The Drop-Off Network

Where the network shows up across Louisville
