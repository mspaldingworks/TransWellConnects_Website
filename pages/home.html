About Transpire

Faith communities showing up for trans people

Transpire is a growing network of trans-affirming congregations and community partners in Louisville. We come from different faith traditions, and we share one commitment: our trans neighbors deserve communities that welcome them, care for them, and stand with them.

Many trans people have been harmed by faith communities instead of welcomed home. Transpire brings congregations together to listen to trans people, learn what they need, and turn good intentions into real change, and to hold one another accountable for it.

You are sacred. You are valued. You belong here.

A project of All People's Justice Center

Transpire is a project of All People's Justice Center, the justice work of All Peoples Unitarian Universalist Congregation in Louisville. The Justice Center builds networks of interfaith congregations and organizations led by the people most affected, and helps congregations and neighbors take part in justice work. Gender justice is one of its focus areas.
