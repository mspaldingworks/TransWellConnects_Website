Welcome. Today, we listen.

For too many transgender people, faith communities have been a source of harm instead of home. Today, transgender speakers share their own paths through faith: leaving, being hurt, finding reconciliation, or still searching. Every faith background is welcome in this room, including none.

For the next ninety minutes, the work of everyone else in the room is to listen.

React as you listen

When something lands, tap how it lands. Only the organizers see the taps, as an anonymous timeline of how the room responded, to reflect on the session afterward.

[transpire_reactions]

Why we’re gathered

  1. To hear what trans people need from faith communities for those communities to become genuinely welcoming and affirming.
  2. To learn what the trans community needs and wants from congregations that say they stand in solidarity.
  3. To move congregations from good intentions to concrete change, and to hold them accountable for it.

How we hold this room

  • Questions are prepared in advance. Speakers have seen every question before today.
  • Speakers set the limits. Anyone can pass on any question, with no explanation needed.
  • A moderator guides the conversation and steps in on any question that is harmful or off-topic.
  • Support is here afterward for the trans folks who take part.

After the session

Tell us what stayed with you and what you will carry back to your congregation or community. Every question is optional, and the form stays open for two weeks.
