Pride and Joy Boxes

Care for the body. Joy for the spirit.

Pride and Joy Boxes are care packages and art boxes in one. Every box carries art and a little sparkle to make queer joy something you can hold. Gender-affirming items go in when you ask for them, in your size. Get one free when you need it, or subscribe.

01 / What’s inside

Two halves of one box.

Every box has the joy half. The care half is yours to shape: tell us what would help and we pack it. Ask for nothing affirming and you still get a full box.

In every box

Queer joy, packed to share.

Art and play for the days you need a reminder that you are loved, celebrated and not alone.

  • Art prints, postcards and stickers
  • Biodegradable glitter (BioGlitz and friends)
  • Coloring pages, craft kits or something to make
  • Zines and short reads by and for our community
  • A handwritten note

When you ask

Gender-affirming care, in your size.

Pick only what you want. Your request stays between you and the people packing your box.

  • Chest binders
  • Tucking underwear and gaffs
  • Packers
  • Breast forms
  • Makeup in your shades
  • Skincare and shaving supplies

Ask once. We keep you in mind.
