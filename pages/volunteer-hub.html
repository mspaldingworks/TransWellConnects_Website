What you’re signing up for.

Four phases, like a barn raising: sort, stage, celebrate, settle. Browse the shifts here, then pick the ones that fit when you fill out the form. Take two or three if you can.

04 / How we show up

Trans-affirming guidelines.

A shared posture, not a rulebook. Tap each to read; we’ll walk through them together at orientation.

01Pronouns first.

Share yours when you introduce yourself. Don’t assume anyone else’s. If you slip, correct yourself and move on without overcorrecting.

02No applications, no questions.

We don’t ask attendees their identity, income, or reason for being here. Everything is theirs. Full stop.

03Affirming language.

Skip “sir” and “ma’am.” Use “friend,” “neighbor,” or someone’s name when you know it.

04Mind the emotional load.

Trans volunteers should not carry the bulk of emotional labor. Spread it across the team — that’s the point of building this together.

05Escalate, don’t engage.

If someone behaves disruptively or asks invasive questions, route them to the coordinator or Safety & Care. Don’t debate.

06Confidentiality.

What happens at the market stays at the market. Don’t photograph attendees without explicit consent.

Take care of yourself

  • Take your full break. Hydrate.
  • Need to step away? Tell your shift lead and go.
  • Witness something difficult? Debrief with the coordinator before leaving.

What to bring

  • Closed-toe shoes (setup & tear-down)
  • Water bottle
  • Mask if you’d like one
  • Open heart, patient hands
